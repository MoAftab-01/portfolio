02 MULTI-AGENT · JOB INTELLIGENCE ↗

AI Job Application Copilot

Multi-agent job intelligence platform designed to automate job discovery, analysis, matching and application workflows. A five-agent LangGraph workflow combines a Profile Analyst, JD Analyzer, Matcher, Resume Optimizer and Cover Letter Generator.

LangGraph Hybrid RAG XGBoost FastAPI LLMs FAISSBM25ReactPostgreSQLDockerAWS
